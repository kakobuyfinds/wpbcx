The shortlist method

How to use Kakobuy finds with more confidence.

A useful shortlist records why an item fits your needs. Keep the product link and the evidence together, then compare before committing.

Start browsing ↗

  1. Choose one category and a clear purpose.

    Begin with an everyday sneaker, a light jacket or a matching set. A specific use makes it easier to compare relevant alternatives.

  2. Record the exact product and variant.

    Save shopping agent product links alongside the seller, color, size and selected option. Confirm seller availability and compatibility with your chosen purchasing service.

  3. Check measurements and visual evidence.

    Compare the clothing size chart or shoe measurements with something you own. Use seller photo comparison and quality control photos, when available, to identify visible differences.

  4. Estimate the complete cost.

    Add the item price, domestic shipping costs, service fees and an international shipping estimate. Include applicable taxes and optional services before setting your final budget.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A quick reference for links, sizing, authenticity and costs.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is typically a curated table of product finds intended for shoppers using Kakobuy. It may collect product links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. The depth and accuracy of those details depend on the person maintaining the list.

Is this page an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide linking to CAFANS categories. It does not represent Kakobuy or any featured brand, and it is not a downloadable spreadsheet.

Does a spreadsheet listing prove a product is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, seller image or quality control photo does not establish authenticity. Check seller identity, provenance and applicable buyer protections independently.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

For shoes, compare the seller’s stated sizing method and measurements with a pair that fits you. For clothing, compare garment measurements such as chest width, length, waist and inseam with an item you already own. Do not rely on a letter size alone.

Does the listed product price include international shipping?

Do not assume that it does. Check the listing and checkout breakdown for domestic shipping, service fees, international delivery, taxes and any optional services. Final costs depend on the order and destination.

Where do the category links on this page lead?

Each category link opens its corresponding CAFANS category. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage. Check the destination listing for current product details and availability.
